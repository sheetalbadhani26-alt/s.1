Jhangora ki kheer

  • MINI 1
  • MINI 2
  • MINI 3

    • Jhangora ki kheer is my favourite dish. Its very famous in tehri garhwal uttrakhand. It is a delicious and creamy dessert made with jhangora (barnyard millet) and milk.

    Ingredients:

    • 1 cup jhangora (barnyard millet)
    • 2 liters milk
    • 1 tablespoon sugar
    • chopped almonds
    • chopped cashews
    • cardamon powder

    ingredients

    1. wash the jhangora first
    2. boil the milk and add the jhangora
    3. add sugar and ghee
    4. cook until the kheer thickens
    5. finally, add chopped nuts and cardamon powder

    important: stir the kheer regularly to prevent it from sticking to the pan.
